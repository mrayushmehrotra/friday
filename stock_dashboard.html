📘 Strategy Logic & Execution Rules

1. Momentum Overextension

Stocks moving up for 5 consecutive sessions experience strong bullish momentum and frequently become overbought on low intraday timeframes.

2. Put Option Price Crush

Continuous bullish days cause dramatic call premium inflation and severe put premium deflation. Out-of-the-money puts become exceptionally cheap (under ₹20 per share).

3. Asymmetric Risk/Reward (≤ ₹5,000)

Max loss is strictly limited to 1 lot capital investment (under ₹5,000). If mean reversion triggers or a pullback occurs, cheap puts can deliver 150%–300%+ returns with minimal initial risk.
